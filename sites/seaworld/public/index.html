    9 AM decisions

    VelociCoaster or Hagrid's first?
    • VelociCoaster first (pick): the ride you came for at its shortest line, riding together. Hagrid's line grows fast after, and it often opens late or breaks down.
    • Hagrid's first: catches its shortest line of the day. If it opens late or stops, you lose the first hour. VelociCoaster later is long unless you split up for single rider.
    • Split option: Hagrid's first, then VelociCoaster single rider.
    Phones and loose items for VelociCoaster
    • VelociCoaster lockers (pick): free, at the entrance, metal detector there for everyone. Short locker line at 9 AM.
    • Hulk lockers: far side of the park, long walk each way, free time may run out and cost money.
    • Hagrid's lockers: close, handy if you ride Hagrid's first, but you walk back before VelociCoaster. Same free-time limit.
    • Phones in the car: fastest through the detector. No entry if passes are only in the app, no wait times, no mobile food, no photos. Car is 15 to 20 min away.
    • Middle option: one phone plus pass cards and IDs, that one phone goes in the VelociCoaster locker.

    Where to eat

    Lunch in the park
    1. Voyager's Smokehouse (pick): ribs, brisket and smoked chicken. Order ahead in the app to skip the line.
    2. Expedition Café: in Antarctica, wide menu. Handy around Penguin Trek and Expedition Odyssey.
    3. Altitude Burgers: smash burgers.
    4. Seafire Grill: grilled and breaded chicken, app ordering.
    Dinner in the park
    1. Sharks Underwater Grill (pick): sit-down next to the shark tank, best-rated in the park. Book a same-day table in the SeaWorld app from 11 AM.
    2. Holiday food booths: open at 4 PM for Christmas Celebration.
    3. Lakeside Grill: on the Waterfront, close to the fireworks.

    The All-Day Dining wristband covers Voyager's, Expedition Café, Altitude Burgers, Seafire Grill and Lakeside Grill, not Sharks. It's on Black Friday sale, so it may beat 15% off if you eat twice. Chick-fil-A usually closes on Thanksgiving.

    Korean BBQ and Asian, within 20 min
    1. KPOT Korean BBQ & Hot Pot, Kirkman Rd (pick): all-you-can-eat KBBQ and hot pot, 4.9 on Google. Normally open until 11:30 PM, kitchen until 10:30, so it works after the fireworks. About 15 min.
    2. SURAH Korean BBQ: 4.8 on Google, table grills and staff help you cook. Holiday hours 4:30 to 9 PM today, so you'd skip Miracles and the fireworks. About 17 min, takes reservations.
    3. KPOT, Palm Pkwy: same menu and hours as the Kirkman one. About 16 min.
    4. Volcano Hot Pot & BBQ, I-Drive: KBBQ and hot pot, normally open until 10:30 PM. About 15 min.
    5. BaBbi BaBbi Korean Kitchen: bibimbap and soondubu, normally open until 9 PM. About 10 min.
    6. Tapanomi Izakaya: Japanese small plates and ramen, normally 6 to 10 PM, walk-in only. About 16 min.

    Only SURAH has posted holiday hours. Call the others before you drive. Closed today: U Turn Korean BBQ, Capybara Ramen. bb.q Chicken closes at 6 PM.

    Other dinner outside the park

    2. Delmonico's Italian Steakhouse: 3 min away, normally open until 11 PM. Call ahead, holiday hours not listed.
    3. Bahama Breeze (I-Drive): normally open until 1 AM. Call ahead.
