    Express from Hard Rock

    Your room keys are your Express passes
    • Where: Universal Studios and Islands of Adventure only. Not Epic Universe, not Volcano Bay.
    • When: Universal's Hard Rock page says from when the keys are issued until the parks close on check-out day. So Saturday morning through Sunday close, for both of you.
    • Not during early entry: Express works in regular park hours only.
    • No Express line: Hagrid's and Pteranodon Flyers. Do Hagrid's at 8 AM early entry.
    • Express works: VelociCoaster, Hulk, Spider-Man, Kong, Forbidden Journey, Gringotts, Hogwarts Express (your 2-park passes cover it), Mummy, Rip Ride Rockit and most others.

    Express rules from Universal's Express Pass page and your Hard Rock check-in email. Get your keys early: Universal staff say you can finish check-in early and pick up keys before the room is ready.
